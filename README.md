## About

Rayanak is an independent app studio. We make simple, ad-light apps that people enjoy using together: party games for get-togethers and learning tools for kids. Our apps are available in English and Spanish, and they work offline wherever possible.

## Our apps

### 🎭 Pantomania
**Charades party game for teams: act it out with no words and no sounds!**

One player acts out a word, their team guesses, and the clock is ticking. Pick a category and a difficulty (1, 2 or 3 points), guess fast for a time bonus, and watch out for fouls.

- 2 to 8 teams, as many rounds as you like
- Hundreds of words in many categories, plus Speedy and Proverbs modes
- English and Spanish
- Works offline, one phone for the whole group
- Free with ads; Premium removes all ads with a one-time purchase

### ⭐ Spelling Star
**Spelling practice for children learning English words.**

Kids practise the spelling of their school words in short, encouraging sessions.

- Practice organised in units
